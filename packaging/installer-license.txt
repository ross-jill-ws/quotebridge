QuoteBridge for MetaTrader

MetaTrader is the registered trademark of MetaQuotes Ltd. QuoteBridge for
MetaTrader is an independent open-source project, not affiliated with or
endorsed by MetaQuotes Ltd.

The installer adds QuoteBridge for your Windows user only and needs no
administrator rights. It contains the QuoteBridge EA as source code and no
compiled EA: the app compiles the EA with each terminal's own MetaEditor.

Source code: https://github.com/ross-jill-ws/quotebridge
